A little closer wins.

Use your physical 0–100 question cards. Everyone, including the reader, writes down a guess before the correct answer is revealed.

  1. Enter each player's guess, from 0 to 100.
  2. Reveal and enter the correct answer.
  3. Points equal the absolute difference. An exact answer scores −10 points.
  4. Subtotal every 7 questions. Lowest total after 21 questions wins. Mini ends after 7.

Negative totals are allowed. Equal lowest totals share the win.
